# Privacy

cenk-overtime is a set of instructions and a few scripts that run on your computer. Its author receives no data from it: there are no servers, hooks, analytics or tracking.

- Reads: the files, exports and repositories you agree to, and your own sent messages found through connectors you have connected.
- Stores: your profile, `profile.json` in the cenk folder (`My Drive/cenk/` in your Google Drive, or `~/.claude/cenk/` without one), kept by the plugin cenk-base and shared with the other cenk-* plugins: your name and its spellings, personal email and phone, your Google account, your jobs' dates, your lawyers' contact details, and their earlier values in its `history`. Never your address, identity numbers, pay or passwords. It stays out of every case folder and is never shared. After your yes, your Claude settings name its folder as `CENK_HOME`. A case folder in your own Google Drive, or on your computer: copies of your evidence, their SHA-256 hashes, a SQLite database and reports. For a large mail archive also a search index of its messages, unmasked, in the case's `work` folder. Earlier versions of the package, moved out of the evidence folder at each delivery, in `work/package`, until you delete them. With the coordinator: the list of your cases (names, folders, states), `cenk-overtime/cases.md` in the cenk folder, and per case folder `.claude/settings.local.json`, which turns the plugin on there.
- Shares: only the case's evidence folder (`<case>-evidence`), with the lawyers you name: your evidence as received, unmasked, its hashes, the reports, and a note for the lawyers only that names open points by record id and kind, never by content; the lawyers can add their own documents in its `6_From_Lawyer` folder. The database, git tables, the mail index and working files are not shared.
- Installs: free programs and Python packages from their official sources (winget, Homebrew, apt, PyPI, the Tesseract project on GitHub), only after your yes. No case data goes with these downloads.
- Employer repositories: cloned with your own git login into `~/overtime-clones/` on your computer, never into the case folder, and deleted after reading unless you ask to keep them.
- Passwords found in your messages: listed in `secrets.json` next to the clones, outside the case folder, used only to mask them, and deleted when you say the package is final.
- Who sees your data: you; Google, which stores your Drive; the lawyers you share the evidence folder with; Anthropic, which processes your Claude sessions under its own privacy policy (https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy); the providers of the connectors you use.
- Other people's data: your records contain colleagues' names and messages. For a large mail archive, the search index in the unshared `work` folder holds every message in it, other people's too, unmasked; its search results (sender, recipients, subject, attachment names, and short excerpts when needed) appear in your Claude session. Delete `work/mail-index` once the package is final; Google Drive may keep earlier versions of a file in its version history. The analysis keeps only your own messages and commits, counts other git authors without storing their addresses, and masks other people's private information before the package is built. The evidence files stay unmasked in `<case>-evidence/4_Raw_Data/`, which your lawyers see.
- Retention: the plugin keeps nothing itself. Your case folder stays until you delete it.
- To delete everything: delete the case folder in Google Drive and empty the Drive trash; delete `~/overtime-clones/<case>/` if anything is left there.
- Your profile: if no other cenk-* plugin uses it, delete `profile.json` in the cenk folder (`My Drive/cenk/` or `~/.claude/cenk/`), an old `claude/profile.json` left from an earlier version, and `CENK_HOME` in the `env` block of your Claude `settings.json`. If another plugin still uses it, ask Claude to erase only what this plugin added (`employment.*`, `contacts.lawyer-*`, `person.name_variants`): erasing a value also removes it from the file's `history`. Google Drive keeps earlier versions of the file for up to 30 days; delete them under the file's Manage versions. The case list: delete the `cenk-overtime` folder in the cenk folder.
